Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few common questions — if yours isn't here, please get in touch.

Do I need a referral from my GP?

You don't need a referral to make an appointment, but one from your GP is recommended — it's usually required to claim a Medicare rebate and helps Dr Wong understand your history. Bring any relevant scans or results if you have them.

Will it be covered by Medicare or private health insurance?

Medically indicated vein and vascular assessments and treatments often attract a Medicare rebate, and private health insurance may contribute towards hospital-based procedures. Purely cosmetic treatments generally aren't covered. We'll always explain the expected costs clearly before you go ahead.

Are vein treatments painful, and what is recovery like?

Modern vein treatments are minimally invasive and generally well tolerated, often using only local anaesthetic. Many are walk-in, walk-out, and most people return to their usual activities quickly. Dr Wong will explain what to expect for your specific treatment.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Bring your referral (if you have one), any previous scans or results, and a list of your medications. For vein consultations, loose clothing makes it easier to examine your legs.

What conditions does Dr Wong treat?

Dr Wong cares for the full range of vascular and vein conditions — from varicose and spider veins to peripheral artery disease, aortic aneurysms, carotid artery disease, deep vein thrombosis and dialysis access. Browse our conditions to learn more.
